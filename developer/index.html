Developer of CampusFlow

I built CampusFlow to solve a problem I kept running into every semester — dense, multi-group college timetables that were hard to read at a glance and even harder to navigate between classes. It's a small, privacy-first project: everything runs in your browser, nothing is ever sent to a server, and it's free to use and adapt. Thanks for checking it out.
